Complex Description

Introducing 420 Kent, a unique living experience directly on the East River in Brooklyn, NY. Composed of three multi-dimensional glass towers designed by ODA New York, 420 Kent will feature 857 residential apartments, 20,000 sf of retail space, over 25,000 sf of indoor amenities, and 80,000 sf of outdoor space including a 400 foot long waterfront esplanade.
